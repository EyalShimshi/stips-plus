## Stips+ 0.24: your own feeds, profile photos that save, and lots more

**Your own feeds**
- A new **Feeds** tab. Stips' categories are a fixed list, but every tag has its own questions: a feed brings up to 15 tags together into one list, newest first, hottest (this week or month), or **mixed** (one from each tag in turn). Leave tags out, or show only questions no one has answered yet.
- Swipe between your feeds. Start from a ready-made one (love and relationships, the army, driving and cars, money and work, school and exams), or make your own, with any emoji.
- **Long-press any #tag** in a question (or the button on a tag's page) to add it to a feed, or to start one with it.
- **Feeds open at once:** your phone keeps what each tag brought and brings in what's new behind it, and while the app is open it keeps your feeds up to date in the background, with requests to spare. A slow tag never holds a feed up; one Stips can't read is skipped and named.
- **Settings → Feeds:** your feeds on Home as chips, in your order, and **share a feed as a link** (on Android 12 and newer it opens straight in Stips+).

**Your profile photo**
- Changing it works now (it never did): pick a photo, drag and pinch it inside the circle, and save. Stips+ checks that Stips really kept it.

**Questions and answers**
- *Copy text* in any answer's ⋮ menu, or as a swipe you pick in Settings.
- Your own answers say **You**, anonymous ones too, and so do quotes of them. Anonymous answers and questions can be detached from your account, as on the site.
- A question you pinned shows its pin filled in your accent color. Pinning or unpinning with a swipe, and turning a question's updates on or off, can be undone from the message (*Undo*).
- Sharing a question shares just its link.

**Pictures and links**
- A picture link (imgbb) is just a *Show image* button. The picture viewer's ⋮ copies or shares the picture, shares its link, or opens it in the browser.
- **Links in pen-pal posts open:** pictures in the viewer, Stips links in the app, the rest in the browser.

**Home and settings**
- Under the hot questions, a **New | Topics** switch, whatever Home's layout. "Like the website" is now *חמות, ואז נושאים (כמו באתר)*.
- Settings → Account → *Profile photo and bio* opens your profile.
- Switching the language to Hebrew and back to the phone's no longer leaves the app left-to-right.

**Updates and news**
- New versions come out as **test builds** first. Settings → About → *Test builds* gets them early (they may have bugs); everyone else gets each version once it's released.
- Now and then a short announcement from Stips+'s maker may pop up, once; Settings → About → *Last announcement* shows it again.
