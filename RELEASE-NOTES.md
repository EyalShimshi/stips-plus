## Stips+ 0.25: voice notes, a chats lock and a privacy screen

**Voice notes**
- A Vocaroo recording (a `voca.ro` link) in a question, an answer, a chat or a pen-pal post plays right in the app, the way a voice message does in WhatsApp: ▶, a bar to move through it, and 1×, 1.5× or 2×. Nothing loads until you press ▶. When the link is all there is, you see just the player.

**Privacy**
- **Lock chats** (Settings → Privacy and safety): your chats open only with your fingerprint, face or screen lock, and lock again after the time you pick away from the app. Meanwhile the chats widget shows who wrote, not what.
- **Hide in recent apps** and **Block screenshots**, in the same place.
- Your sign-in token is now kept encrypted on the phone, with a key that never leaves it.
- How Stips+ treats your account, in plain words: *How Stips+ protects your account*, also in Privacy and safety.

**Shared feeds**
- A feed's link opens the feed itself: its questions, and *Add to my feeds* on top. You can try its order first. In a chat, the link shows as a card.

**Also**
- You can check every download: each APK comes with GitHub's signed record of where it was built, and the signing certificate is listed on the site. SECURITY.md on GitHub says how.
- About → **Thanks**: Fallen, for security and privacy advice.
- The sign-in screen says exactly where your password goes, and the app now says plainly that it comes as is, to be used at your own risk.
